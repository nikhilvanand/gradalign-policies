Gradalign

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Gradalign is an app that helps academies (martial arts, dance, music, sport, fitness and others) run attendance, fees, progress reports, staff and notices. It is operated by Nikhil Anand, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

In short: your academy decides what goes into Gradalign about its students. We store it to run the app for the academy, families and staff, we don't sell it, and we don't use it for ads.

Who is responsible for your data

Each academy that uses Gradalign decides which students, guardians and staff it adds and what records it keeps. The academy is responsible for that data (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, it is the "data fiduciary"). We process it on the academy's behalf to provide the service. For questions about a particular student's records, you can contact your academy or us.

What we collect

Account details. When you sign in with Google or an email link: your email address, and your name and profile picture link if Google provides them.

Student records, added by the academy. Student name, date of birth, gender, admission date, branch and batch, current level or belt, and the parent or guardian's name, relationship, email and phone number. For event entries, a student's weight.

Health and fitness, added by the academy and its specialists. Height and weight measurements, fitness test results and scores, and, for students an academy assigns to a specialist (such as a physiotherapist or nutritionist), that specialist's workout or meal plans and notes.

Activity in the app. Attendance, check-ins at the academy, fee invoices and whether they're paid, progress sheets, assessment and grading results, belt promotions and achievements, coach remarks, notices, and in-app notifications.

Staff details. Name, email, role and branch for academy admins, branch admins, instructors and specialists, and for association administrators. For academy staff: their attendance register, pay settings and salary payments. Academies can also list staff who don't sign in, by name only.

Academy accounts. The academy's own expenses, such as rent and equipment.

Device details. A push notification token for your phone, so we can send you notices and reminders.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. To check in at an academy, the app uses your camera only to scan the QR code on the academy's tablet. No picture is taken or stored.

Payments

When you pay a fee by UPI, Gradalign opens your own UPI app with the academy's UPI ID and amount filled in. The payment happens in your UPI app. We never see or store your bank, card or UPI PIN details. We only record that a fee was marked as paid.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you only the academies, students and records you're allowed to see
  • To run the academy's day-to-day work: attendance and check-ins, fees, progress, grading, events, staff pay and expenses
  • To let assigned specialists write plans and notes for a student, and share them with the student's family and coaches
  • To send notices and reminders, for example fees due or a new progress report
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems

Who can see it

  • Your academy's staff, limited by their role and branch. Staff pay, salary payments and expenses are visible only to academy admins, and staff can see their own attendance and pay.
  • Specialists see only the students the academy assigns to them, and only while assigned
  • Parents and guardians see only their own children's records. Adult students see only their own.
  • Associations that an academy branch has chosen to affiliate with see the event entries submitted to them, and branch-level counts
  • Service providers who host and deliver the app for us: Supabase (database and sign-in, data stored in Japan) and Google Firebase (web hosting and push notifications). They process data only to provide their service to us.

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data if Indian law requires it.

Children

Many students are under 18. Their records are added by the academy with a parent or guardian's consent, which the academy records when it registers the student. Parents and guardians sign in to see their children's records, including any specialist plans and notes. Gradalign isn't directed at children signing up on their own.

Health and fitness information

Measurements, fitness results and specialist plans are used only to support the student's training. Gradalign doesn't give medical advice, and we don't use this information for anything else. See our Terms of Service.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with access rules that limit each person to their own academy, role and students. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep records while the academy uses Gradalign so it can keep its attendance, fee and progress history. If an academy stops using Gradalign, we delete its data within 90 days of a request from the academy. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: ask your academy, or write to us
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings at any time
  • Withdraw consent for a child's records: tell the academy or us, and we'll work with the academy to remove them

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app.
