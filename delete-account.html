Delete your Gradalign account

How to ask

  1. Email nikhilanand2@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Gradalign account". Tell us your academy's name, and whether you also want your children's records removed.
  3. We'll reply to confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account, name, email and profile picture link
  • Your phone's push notification tokens and your in-app notifications
  • Your link to your academy and to any students you were connected to

What the academy may keep

Student records (attendance, fees, progress and belt history) belong to the academy, which may need them for its own records. If you ask us to remove your children's or your own student records, we'll pass the request to the academy and remove them once it confirms. This usually happens within the same 30 days. Records are removed from backups as they are overwritten.
